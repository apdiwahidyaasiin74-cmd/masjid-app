========================================================================
TALAABOOYINKA LAGU WADAJIRO MASHRUUCA SHIINE COMPUTER KALE
========================================================================

1. DATABASE-KA:
   - Fur XAMPP oo shid Apache iyo MySQL.
   - Fur browser-ka oo gal: http://localhost/phpmyadmin
   - Samee Database cusub oo magaciisu yahay: shiine
   - Dooro database-ka 'shiine', kadib riix "Import".
   - Dooro faylka ku dhex jira folder-kan ee magaciisu yahay:
     shiine_database.sql
   - Riix "Go" ama "Import" si dhammaan xogta (shaqaalaha, imaamyada, 
     xubnaha guddiga, permission-yada iyo dejinta fariimaha) ugu shubmaan.

2. FAYLKA .ENV:
   - Folder-kan waxaa ku jira .env iyo .env.backup.
   - Xaqiiji in xogta MySQL ay sax tahay:
     DB_CONNECTION=mysql
     DB_HOST=127.0.0.1
     DB_PORT=3306
     DB_DATABASE=shiine
     DB_USERNAME=root
     DB_PASSWORD=

3. BILAABIDDA APPLICATION-KA:
   - Fur Command Prompt ama PowerShell gudaha folder-kan:
     php artisan key:generate
     php artisan serve
   - Waxaa kaaga furmaya browser-ka: http://127.0.0.1:8000

Dhammaan xogtii iyo koodhkii mashruuca waa kuwo diyaarsan oo aan waxba ka maqnayn!
========================================================================
